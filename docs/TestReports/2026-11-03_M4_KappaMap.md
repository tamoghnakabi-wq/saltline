# Test report — Milestone 4 (Kappa Saltworks map), 2026-11-03

All results below come from runs on 2026-11-03. Nothing here is estimated.

**Hardware:** one Apple Silicon Mac (M4). Every player was a separate game process on that one machine.
**Network:** the processes talked only through **Unity Relay** (transport `RelayUnityTransport`). Each one signed in as its own anonymous Unity player.
**Builds:** the macOS release build. Version 0.1.0, network protocol 3. The latency and lag-compensation tests use the development build; their results are in section 6.
**Not tested yet:**
- Windows players.
- Players on separate machines or networks.
- A human playing the map with mouse and keyboard. Every run here was scripted.
- Frame rate while playing on Kappa (see section 7).

## 1. What was built

**Kappa Saltworks** is an original map: a salt evaporation plant on a cliff above the sea. It was modelled in Blender through the Blender MCP connection, from a Python script (`ArtSource/Blender/kappa_build.py`), and exported as one FBX. Nothing was copied from another game.

- **Size.** The playable area is 116 × 72 m, with backdrop beyond it: warehouses, hillside, cliff and sea.
- **Symmetry.** The map is mirror-symmetric, so both teams get identical routes. Red spawns in the west, Blue in the east.
- **Three lanes:**
  - **North, the salt pans.** Open brine pans with storage tanks for cover, and a sunken channel 1.2 m deep. A footbridge crosses the channel low enough that you must crouch under it, and a sluice gate stands over it. A raised catwalk at 3.0 m has stairs at both ends and from the pans.
  - **Middle, the pump house.** A two-storey concrete building. Its ground floor has the pump, and the upper floor (3.7 m) has windows over the courtyards. Stairs inside lead up.
  - **South, the dock.** Corrugated sheds that bullets pass through, salt piles, a raised dock platform (1.2 m) with ramps and stairs, a crane, and a cliff road with a sea wall.
- **Spawn yards.** Each team spawns in a walled yard (8 spawn points per team) behind an office building. Baffle walls block every sight line into the spawns from outside the yard. The existing spawn-protection timer still applies.
- **Player clips.** Invisible walls on their own `PlayerClip` layer stop players climbing onto roofs and out of bounds. They block movement only; bullets and cameras pass through.
- **Textures.** 17 seamless 1024² texture sets: colour (with a smoothness channel) and normal maps. They are generated from seeded noise by `ArtSource/Textures/gen_kappa_textures.py`, so they are original and can be regenerated.
- **Lighting.** A realtime sun with shadows, a procedural sky, gradient ambient light, a generated reflection cubemap, distance fog and post-processing: bloom, ACES tone mapping, colour grading and vignette. 11 lamps are realtime point lights without shadows.
- **Scene build.** `KappaSceneFactory` imports the FBX and builds the scene. Object name suffixes decide the collider type: none for decor, an invisible clip for `__PC`, a mesh collider plus a surface type (for impact effects) for everything else. Spawn points come from markers in the FBX.
- **Map tour tool.** `SALTLINE -batchmode -maptour Kappa -capture <dir>` renders 17 fixed viewpoints from the built game, for visual review.

Geometry: 232 objects, 27,862 triangles, 168 collidable objects, 12 player clips.

### Problems these tests found, and the fixes

1. **The pump-house stairs could not be climbed.** The bottom step was 0.2 m from a wall, too narrow for a player. Fix: the stairs now climb the other way, starting in the open, with a gap in the railing at the top.
2. **Spawns could be seen from outside.** There were 474 sight lines, through the gap between the office corner and the start of the baffle wall. Fix: the baffles now reach the office wall.
3. **Spawning players faced a wall.** The UI test's screenshot showed this, not a unit test: every spawn faced the office wall 6 m away. Spawns now face the way out of the yard, and the spawn test now checks for open space in front instead of a fixed direction.

The motor walk also showed two things about the test's own model, not the map:
- A single sample per grid cell counts the open side edge of a staircase as floor, but a body centred there slides off.
- A route can climb onto a staircase from its side one tread at a time, while the 0.72 m-wide body meets the next tread too.

The route search now requires floor under the whole footprint and a clear step across the body's width.

**Open-sided stairs.** The catwalk end stairs and the dock side stairs have no side railings, so a player can step off their side. The worst case is a 2.7 m drop from the top of the catwalk stairs. This is allowed movement, not a bug, but it should get a playtest decision.

## 5. Visual review

The map tour renders 17 viewpoints from the release build. The first pass showed four problems, all fixed:
- The asphalt read as stone paving. It is now fine aggregate with dark hairline cracks.
- The hillside was flat with an obviously repeating texture. It now has relief and gullies, a larger texture scale and boulders.
- The top-down view was washed out by fog. Fog is now off for orthographic captures.
- One camera was blocked by a truck.

Screenshots from the final build are in `docs/TestReports/M4_kappa/`. They include in-game views from the UI test: the HUD at spawn, the scoreboard and third person.

## 7. Not measured, and known limits

- **Frame rate on Kappa has not been measured.** The map tour logs 0.5–0.8 ms per `Camera.Render` at 1600×900 on the M4. That is only the CPU time to submit a frame, not GPU time and not a frame rate. A real frame-time capture during play is still to do, including the MacBook Air M4 preset.
- **No baked lighting.** There are no lightmaps, light probes or baked occlusion culling. Interiors are lit by the sun's bounce approximation (gradient ambient) and shadowless point lights.
- **Collision.** Every collidable object uses a non-convex mesh collider. Physics cost has not been profiled.
- **Assets.** The map has no decals, no detail textures and no ambient sound yet. Characters and weapons are still placeholders (milestone 5).
- **Platforms.** Only tested on macOS.
