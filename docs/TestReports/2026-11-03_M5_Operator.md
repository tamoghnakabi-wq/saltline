# Test report: operator character and animation (graphics overhaul), 2026-11-03

All results below come from runs on 2026-11-03 on one Apple Silicon Mac (M4), using the macOS release build (0.1.0, protocol 3). Nothing here is estimated.

**Not tested yet:**
- Windows.
- Separate machines.
- A human playing with mouse and keyboard.
- Frame rate with eight animated operators on screen.

## 1. What was built

- **Operator character** (`ArtSource/Blender/operator_build.py`, built in Blender through the Blender MCP connection). It is an original design:
  - body, balaclava with a soft-edged eye slot, glasses, helmet with rails, ear defenders and NVG mount, plate carrier with pouches, backpack, belt, holster, knee pads and boots;
  - 52-bone Humanoid rig, with rigid weights on hard gear and transferred weights on cloth;
  - one 2048² texture set (albedo, normal, metallic/AO/smoothness mask);
  - three LODs: 50,184 / 25,090 / 11,040 triangles;
  - a team-colour material slot for the armband and helmet patch.
- **Animation.** 49 Adobe Mixamo clips (rifle 8-way locomotion pack plus deaths), imported as Humanoid and retargeted. `AC_Operator` has a 25-clip standing blend tree (idle + walk/run/sprint × 8 directions), a 9-clip crouch tree, an air state and 5 deaths.
- **OperatorRig** (runtime):
  - drives the animator from each player's replicated render pose;
  - scales playback so the feet keep pace with the ground speed;
  - blades and pitches the torso with the aim;
  - holds the weapon along the true aim direction;
  - puts both hands on the weapon with two-bone IK, including the magazine reload, the shotgun pump, sprint low-ready and weapon switching;
  - shifts the body height so the head matches the host's hitboxes.
- **Fallback.** Without the model, players use the old hitbox capsules.

Issues found by looking at the images and fixed:
1. The balaclava rendered as a black void, so the head had no face. It is now charcoal-olive with a soft oval eye opening around the glasses.
2. The crouched, sliding and jumping bodies were 0.3–0.4 m off their hitboxes. The hips now shift and the legs are re-solved with IK, so the feet stay planted.
3. The sprint gun pointed steeply down. It is now held low-ready across the chest.

## 4. Live match over Relay

`Tools/run_relay_tests.sh ui`: **PASS**. Results are in `AutoTest/ui_064222/`, outside the repo.

- **Setup.** Two separate game processes connected only through Unity Relay.
  - The host ran with graphics and drove every screen. It went from the menu through lobby, warm-up and live play to results.
  - The client was a scripted match player.
- **Outcome.** The match ran to its result with kills on both sides. Both processes reported PASS, and neither log contains an exception.
- **The new body in a live match.** In the host's third-person capture (`12_live_tps.png`), the player shows as the operator: red team patch, backpack, weapon held. It is posed by `PlayerView` from the replicated pose, not by the showcase.

This run used the build made just before the IK math moved into `TwoBoneIK.cs`. That change only moved code, and the EditMode tests cover it. The final build was then rebuilt, and the character showcase was re-run on it (`MapTour/char5`).

## 5. Known limitations

- The character is stylised-realistic, made from code in Blender. It is not a photoscanned AAA character. The face is masked, and there are no facial animations.
- There is no foot IK on stairs or slopes, and the turn-in-place clips are not used yet. The body turns with the aim.
- In the bolt-action rifle's cycle, the bolt moves but the right hand stays on the grip.
- There are no first-person arms yet (planned next).
- The Mixamo clips are not in the repository. They cannot be redistributed. See `docs/ANIMATION.md`.
